# Second Sharif Mathematics Journal's Night: Celebrating the Second Issue of the 3rd Series
On July 7, 2024, Sharif Mathematics Journal held an event in celebration of the publication of its second issue. The evening consisted of four explanatory talks, most of them sharing topics with articles published in the corresponding issue.

## Lectures
- _**The Geometry of the Space of Probability Measures**_, by [Amin Talebi](https://math.sharif.ir/faculties/talebi), [Video](https://youtu.be/OyixzhprHSg)
- _**An application of Semi-definite Programming in Quantum Information Theory**_, by [Ali Almasi](https://ali-almasi.github.io), [Lecture script](https://ali-almasi.github.io/assets/talks_materials/An_Application_of_SDP_in_QIT.pdf), [Video](https://youtu.be/TUFU0se7ekQ)
- _**On some of Maryam Mirzakhani's work which resulted in her receiving the Fields medal**_, by [Amir Jafari](https://math.sharif.ir/faculties/ajafari), [Lecture script](/assets/photos/SMJ_Night_2/3_Slides.pdf), [Video](https://youtu.be/Gne6ekoDLMk)
- _**Self-avoiding Walks on the Hexagonal Lattice**_, by Mahla Amiri, [Lecture script](/assets/photos/SMJ_Night_2/4_Slides.pdf), [Video](https://youtu.be/f8yKssxOwLg)
